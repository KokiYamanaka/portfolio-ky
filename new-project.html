Case Study

Cyberfight

Gamified Campaign

The Opportunity

Before Cyberfight holds a major wrestling event, it needs to secure high-value sponsorship revenue. We partnered with the Cyberfight team to build an O2O gamified marketing campaign to secure sponsorship.

The Solution

To secure a high-value sponsorship, we built a digital stamp rally within the LINE app. Each user subscribed to Cyberfight's LINE account who completes all missions (e.g., visits a restaurant) receives an official digital wallpaper of a wrestler.

The result: 350+ users completed missions, satisfying the sponsorship requirements.

My Duties

As Project Manager, I was responsible for three things:

  1. Communicate with the client from requirements to post-release.
  2. Build and test the campaign system end to end with an internal no-code system.
  3. Design the UI/UX with an existing template in Figma.
