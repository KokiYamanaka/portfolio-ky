OVERVIEW

Takeaway

•Learn a framework to grow an Instagram account

•Backed by my actual experience

•And by a leading Instagram marketing agency that helped 500 companies reach an average of 10K followers in 4.2 months

 

OVERVIEW

PECT Framework

•Profile: build a profile that triggers a follow in 3 seconds

•Engagement: turn followers into fans

•Collection: make users save your content instantly

•Traffic: guide users to visit your profile

 

P · PROFILE

Profile

•Aim: trigger a follow within 3 seconds of a profile visit

•Actions: concept design, then profile page creation and improvement

•KPI: follower conversion of 6–8% per month

 

P · PROFILE

Concept Design

•Value proposition delivered to a specific person

•Merit (functional value): “I want thinner legs”

•Benefit (emotional value): “I want to wear cute clothes”

•Combined: “A simple diet method I can follow, so I feel good about my body”

 

P · PROFILE

Profile Page Creation and Improvement

•The account page clearly shows the value the user is looking for

•Use the AIDMA method to craft your profile

•Show a sample account page using your own

 

P · PROFILE

AIDMA

•Attention: what the account is

•Interest: what information you provide

•Desire: what concrete content you offer

•Memory: a summary that lets the target user check it provides what they want

•Action: follow your account

 

E · ENGAGEMENT

Engagement

•Aim: make your posts show up in followers’ timelines

•Actions: Stories and Live

•KPI: home rate of 80% weekly

 

C · COLLECTION

Collection

•Aim: make your posts show up in the Explore tab for non-followers

•Actions: feed posts and Reels

•KPI: home rate of 2% (the day after posting)

 

C · COLLECTION

Reels

•Successful Reels have two qualities

•Retention: most people watch until the end

•Collection: a high save rate

 

C · COLLECTION

Retention

•Two principles make most people watch until the end

•Storytelling: hook, conflict, tension, payoff

•Broad-to-niche hook

 

C · COLLECTION

Storytelling

•Hook: within 3 seconds, give the general idea and mention the payoff; be concise

•Conflict: introduce the central struggle that opposes a character’s or creator’s goal

•Tension: create back-and-forth struggles so the viewer can’t scroll away

•Payoff: deliver the final resolution that fulfills the promise made by the hook

 

C · COLLECTION

Broad-to-Niche Hook

•Craft your hook so most people understand it

•It largely decides whether your Reel reaches massive views

•Low views: a hook about specific technical knowledge

•High views: a fast-paced edit of the recent F1 race in Singapore, visuals only and no language

 

C · COLLECTION

Collection (Saves)

•Reels get saved because they either entertain or educate

•Subjectivity: a photo of your personal room > Ikea furniture

•Look-back value: a step-by-step recipe > watching yourself cook; outfit checks work because people want to buy the same outfit

•Be clear on the value proposition of each Reel

 

T · TRAFFIC

Traffic

•Aim: trigger people to visit your profile after watching your Reels

•Action: guide viewers to your profile page

•KPI: profile access rate of 1% (checked biweekly)

 

SUMMARY

Summary

•Continuously improve all four components

•Follower growth becomes consistently faster

•A strong framework that applies to many types of accounts, from restaurant marketing to software products
