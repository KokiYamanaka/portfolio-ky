Case Study

Novatrade

Awareness and Traffic Generation

The Opportunity

Novatrade aims to sell its existing technical courses to consumers and needed a marketing channel to build awareness. I partnered with the team to build an Instagram channel that drives viewers to the courses.

The Solution

We produced high-retention short-form videos that teach startup tactics from notable founders' stories to business owners and engineers.

The Result

  • 5 consecutive reels reached 2-3.6k views, with one breakout reel hitting 19.6k views (2% save rate, 32% follow rate).
  • Weekly CTR from reels to the bio page rose from 1% to 2.5% over 4 weeks.
